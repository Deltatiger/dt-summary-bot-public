Personal & self-hosted

Turn selected Gmail messages into short Discord summaries.

DT Summary Bot is a small, non-commercial automation that watches for email from a short list of senders, creates a readable summary, and delivers it to a private Discord channel. It is built and run by one person for personal use — there is no sign-up, no public dashboard, and no advertising.

What it does

The bot connects to a single Gmail account that its owner controls. It looks only at unread messages that match a configured list of senders — for example, a daily news digest or a market report. Each matching message is parsed, summarised, and posted to a Discord channel through a private webhook. After the summary is delivered, the email is marked as read so it is not processed again.

1

Reads what it is told to

Only unread mail from senders on the owner's configured allow-list is retrieved. Other email is never fetched or processed.

2

Summarises with AI

Message content is sent to a trusted AI provider to produce a concise summary. The summary retains the important details of the original.

3

Delivers to Discord

The summary is posted to a private Discord channel owned by the account holder. Nothing is posted publicly.

4

Marks as read

The original email is marked as read to prevent duplicate notifications. No messages are sent, deleted, or archived.

How Google user data is used

With the account owner's explicit consent, the app uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access Gmail. The only permission requested is gmail.modify, which is used to read matching messages and mark them as read. Google user data is never sold, rented, or used for advertising or profiling.

Limited Use disclosure

DT Summary Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For full details on what is accessed, how it is stored, who it is shared with, and how to delete it, see the Privacy Policy.
